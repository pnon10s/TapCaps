⭐ Tap Caps v3.1 — Release Notes

New
Newlines "`n" are allowed in Symbol Sets to help visually organize the characters. The newlines are shown in the palette as the "↵" symbol but do not cause a row break. The "↵" is then converted back to newline when the set is saved.

⭐ Tap Caps v3.0 — Release Notes

New
Added a command to cycle through three control modes based on user preference. Choose Full Control Mode for complete access to all selection methods and commands, or switch to one of the two Quick Pick Modes for fast, low‑effort symbol pasting with minimal palette interaction.

The palette now reappears exactly where you last positioned it, making Tap Caps feel stable and predictable across sessions.

Improved
The status line now reflects the current mode and user expectations more accurately, reducing confusion and making Tap Caps feel more responsive.

Various small refinements to keep palette behavior smooth, consistent, and intuitive.
